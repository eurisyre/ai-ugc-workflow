# AI UGC Production Workflow

## Step 1: Product Analysis
Identify product category, key benefit, target audience, and visual selling point.

## Step 2: Shot Planning
Create 4 core UGC shots: wide, medium, close-up, and texture.

## Step 3: Prompt Creation
Generate image prompts and image-to-video prompts.

## Step 4: Content Packaging
Create hook, CTA, caption, and platform-ready output.
